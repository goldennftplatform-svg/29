# Safari artwork and country boards

Visual direction: the user's cobalt-blue giraffe painting and gold/coral/ink lion painting supplied in chat. Original image files were not available in the repository; they have NOT been recreated or claimed as embedded. Add the original JPG/PNG files under `assets/art/` to complete the branded hero/table artwork pass. The current CSS supplies the palette and painted-mark motif, not replacement illustrations.

Country outlines: simplified Natural Earth coordinates from https://github.com/johan/world.geo.json/tree/master/countries (KEN, TZA, ZAF, MDG). Mainland/exterior contours are used for gameplay. South Africa's Lesotho enclave is drawn separately. These are stylized game boards, not boundary reference maps.

Each board has 120 equally spaced score positions plus 121/win and a distinct score-zero start. Each player has an inset colored lane. The selected country is a browser preference and doesn't change scoring rules.

PFPs: explicit upload only; JPEG/PNG/WebP decoded and cropped to a 192px JPEG. Saved in browser storage, rendered at the local player's seat and scoreboard. Opponents have empty portrait slots; portrait networking and generated avatars are not implemented.
